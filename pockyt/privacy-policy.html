Pockyt Privacy Policy

Your financial data, your privacy.

1. Information We Collect

Pockyt stores your financial data — transactions, cards, budgets, goals, recurring entries, subscriptions, and bill splits — locally on your device. Pro subscribers may enable optional cloud sync, which stores your data in Firebase Firestore to enable cross-device access. We do not sell or share your personal financial data with third parties.

2. AI Features

Pockyt supports AI-powered receipt scanning and a conversational AI assistant. These features require you to supply your own API key from a provider of your choice:

  • OpenAI (ChatGPT)
  • Google Gemini
  • Anthropic Claude
  • DeepSeek

When you use an AI feature, relevant data (receipt images, transaction context, or your chat messages) is sent directly to your chosen provider under that provider's privacy policy. Pockyt does not route, store, or log this data on its own servers. Your API key is stored locally on your device and is never transmitted to Pockyt's servers.

3. Authentication

Pockyt uses Firebase Authentication. You may use the app entirely without signing in — all core features work offline with local storage. You may optionally sign in with Google, Apple, or email/password to enable cloud sync and cross-device access.

4. Camera & Photos

Pockyt requests camera and photo library access solely for receipt capture. Receipt images are processed on-device or sent to your configured AI provider for OCR; they are not stored by Pockyt's servers. You may attach receipt photos to transactions; these are stored locally on your device.

5. Notifications

Pockyt may send local push notifications to remind you of upcoming credit card payment due dates and recurring transaction reminders. These notifications are scheduled entirely on-device and no notification content is transmitted to any server.

6. Local-Only Features

The following features compute everything on your device and send no data externally:

  • Spending heatmap and category breakdowns
  • Cash flow forecast
  • Bill splitting
  • Subscription management
  • Merchant auto-categorization rules
  • Cashback optimizer hints
  • CSV import and PDF export
  • Budget tracking and savings goals

7. Data Security

Cloud-synced data is protected by Firebase security rules that ensure only you can access your account. You can delete all your cloud data at any time from Settings. Local data can be cleared by deleting the app.

8. Regional Availability

Pockyt operates globally. Cloud sync and AI features may be restricted or unavailable in mainland China due to local data protection and internet regulations. We are working to bring compliant versions of these features to users in China.

9. Bank Integration (Coming Soon)

A future update will allow you to securely link bank accounts for automatic transaction import via a regulated open banking API with read-only access. Pockyt will never store your banking credentials. A full privacy policy update will be provided before this feature launches.

10. Contact

For questions, feedback, or data deletion requests, please contact us at wksunshine@gmail.com.

Pockyt 隐私政策

您的财务数据，由您掌控。

1. 我们收集的信息

Pockyt 将您的财务数据（包括交易记录、信用卡、预算、储蓄目标、定期条目、订阅及账单分摊）本地存储在您的设备上。Pro 订阅用户可选择启用云同步功能，将数据安全存储在 Firebase Firestore 中，以实现跨设备访问。我们不会向第三方出售或共享您的个人财务数据。

2. AI 功能

Pockyt 支持 AI 收据扫描和对话式 AI 助手。使用这些功能需要您自行提供所选服务商的 API 密钥：

  • OpenAI（ChatGPT）
  • Google Gemini
  • Anthropic Claude
  • DeepSeek

使用 AI 功能时，相关数据（收据图片、交易上下文或对话内容）将直接发送至您所选的服务商，并受该服务商隐私政策的约束。Pockyt 不会在自身服务器上转发、存储或记录这些数据。您的 API 密钥仅存储在本地设备上，不会传输至 Pockyt 服务器。

3. 身份验证

Pockyt 使用 Firebase Authentication 进行身份验证。您无需登录即可使用所有核心功能，数据完全离线存储在本地。您也可以选择通过 Google、Apple 或电子邮件/密码登录，以启用云同步和跨设备访问。

4. 相机与照片

Pockyt 仅为拍摄收据请求相机和相册访问权限。收据图片在设备本地处理，或发送至您所配置的 AI 服务商进行文字识别；Pockyt 服务器不会存储这些图片。附加到交易记录的收据照片存储在您的本地设备上。

5. 通知

Pockyt 可能会发送本地推送通知，提醒您即将到来的信用卡还款日和定期交易提醒。这些通知完全在设备本地调度，不会向任何服务器传输通知内容。

6. 纯本地功能

以下功能仅在您的设备上进行计算，不向外部发送任何数据：

  • 消费热力图及分类统计
  • 现金流预测
  • 账单分摊
  • 订阅管理
  • 商家自动分类规则
  • 返现优化提示
  • CSV 导入及 PDF 导出
  • 预算追踪及储蓄目标

7. 数据安全

云端同步数据受 Firebase 安全规则保护，确保只有您本人才能访问自己的账户。您可随时在"设置"中删除所有云端数据。本地数据可通过卸载应用清除。

8. 地区可用性

Pockyt 面向全球用户提供服务。由于当地数据保护和互联网法规，云同步及 AI 功能在中国大陆可能受到限制或无法使用。我们正积极推进合规版本的开发，以服务中国用户。

9. 银行集成（即将推出）

未来版本将支持通过受监管的开放银行 API 以只读方式安全关联银行账户，实现自动导入交易记录。Pockyt 绝不会存储您的银行凭据。功能上线前，我们将提供完整的隐私政策更新说明。

10. 联系方式

如有疑问、反馈或数据删除请求，请发送邮件至 wksunshine@gmail.com。
