LymphaLight Privacy Policy

Your health data stays on your device.

LymphaLight is local-first. All your health data is stored only on your device. There is no account, no login, and no server.

1. Information We Collect

LymphaLight stores the following data locally on your device only, using AsyncStorage:

  • Onboarding information: age range, lymphoma diagnosis type, and treatment status
  • Daily symptom check-ins: night sweats, temperature, weight, itching, alcohol-induced pain, and optional notes
  • Lymph node logs: region, size measurements, and how each node feels (firmness, mobility, tenderness)
  • App settings: reminder time, text size, high-contrast mode, and reduce-motion preferences

This data never leaves your device. LymphaLight has no backend server, no database, and no analytics.

2. No Account or Login Required

LymphaLight does not require you to create an account or sign in. No email address, phone number, or personal identifiers are collected.

3. Backup & Restore

You can export all your data as a JSON file and restore it on another device by pasting the backup. This export is initiated entirely by you and handled locally on your device. The exported file may contain your health information — please treat it with appropriate care.

4. PDF Reports

You can generate a PDF summary of your check-ins and node history to share with your care team. PDF generation and sharing (via the system share sheet) is handled on-device by iOS/Android. Pockyt's servers are not involved.

5. Notifications

LymphaLight can send a daily local notification to remind you to complete your check-in. Notifications are scheduled entirely on-device via expo-notifications. No notification data is sent to any server.

6. Third-Party Services

LymphaLight does not integrate any analytics, advertising, crash-reporting, or third-party tracking SDKs. No data is shared with any third party.

7. Data Deletion

You can delete all your data at any time from Settings → Clear All Data. Uninstalling the app removes all locally stored data. There is no cloud copy to delete.

8. Children's Privacy

LymphaLight is not directed at children under 13. If you believe a child has entered personal health information, please use the Clear All Data option in Settings.

9. Contact

For questions or feedback, please contact us at wksunshine@gmail.com.

LymphaLight is intended to support self-tracking and conversations with your healthcare providers. It is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Always consult a qualified healthcare professional about your health.
