Oncora Privacy Policy

Your health log stays on your device.

Oncora is local-first. Your health log is stored only on your device — there is no account and no login. Two optional features (the AI companion and ads) connect to the internet; they are described below.

1. Information We Collect

Oncora stores the following data locally on your device only, using on-device storage (AsyncStorage):

  • Onboarding information: age range, cancer group and subtype, diagnosis details, and treatment status
  • Daily symptom check-ins and the tailored questions for your cancer type, plus energy, mood, and optional notes
  • Lymph node logs: region, size measurements, and how each node feels (firmness, mobility, tenderness)
  • Photos you add (lymph nodes, skin, wounds, swelling) — stored on your device only
  • Treatments and medications, lab results, side-effects, appointments, care-team contacts, and questions for your doctor
  • App settings: reminder times, units, language, text size, high-contrast, reduce-motion, and app-lock preferences

This health log is not uploaded to us. Oncora has no account system and we do not collect your name, email, phone number, or other personal identifiers.

2. No Account or Login Required

Oncora does not require you to create an account or sign in to track your health. We never sell your information.

3. AI Companion

Oncora includes an optional AI companion. When you send it a message, that message is transmitted to our server, which relays it to a third-party AI provider to generate a reply. Please avoid including details you would not want processed this way. If you do not use the AI companion, no messages are sent. We do not use your stored health log to train any AI model.

4. Advertising

Oncora may display banner ads supplied by Google AdMob on some screens. We request non-personalized ads only — they are not based on tracking you across apps or on your health data. To serve and measure ads, AdMob may process limited technical data such as your device's IP address and general device information. We do not show ads during the check-in flow or the AI companion. See Google's Privacy Policy for how AdMob handles data.

5. Backup & Restore

You can export all your data as a JSON file and restore it on another device by pasting the backup or choosing a backup file. Exports and the automatic on-device snapshots are handled locally on your device. An exported file may contain your health information — please treat it with appropriate care once it leaves the app.

6. Reports You Choose To Share

You can generate a PDF summary or a caregiver update to share with your care team or family. These are created on-device and only leave the app via the system share sheet when you explicitly choose to share them.

7. Notifications

Oncora can send local reminders for check-ins, appointments, medications, and the mid-cycle (nadir) blood-count alert. Notifications are scheduled entirely on-device. No notification data is sent to any server.

8. Data Deletion

You can delete individual entries at any time within the app. Uninstalling the app removes all locally stored data from your device. There is no cloud copy of your health log to delete.

9. Children's Privacy

Oncora is not directed at children under 13. If you believe a child has entered personal health information, please remove it within the app or uninstall the app.

10. Contact

For questions or feedback, please contact us at wksunshine@gmail.com.

Oncora is intended to support self-tracking and conversations with your healthcare providers. It is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Always consult a qualified healthcare professional about your health.

Oncora 隱私政策

您的健康紀錄只留在您的裝置上。

Oncora 採用本機優先設計。您的健康紀錄僅儲存於您的裝置上——沒有帳號，也無需登入。有兩項選用功能（AI 夥伴與廣告）會連上網路，詳見下文。

1. 我們收集的資訊

Oncora 僅使用裝置本機儲存（AsyncStorage）將以下資料儲存於您的裝置本機：

  • 初始設定資訊：年齡範圍、癌症類別與亞型、診斷細節及治療狀態
  • 每日症狀記錄與您癌症類型的客製問題，以及精力、情緒及選填備註
  • 淋巴結紀錄：部位、尺寸測量值，以及各淋巴結的觸感（硬度、活動度、壓痛）
  • 您新增的照片（淋巴結、皮膚、傷口、腫脹）——僅儲存於您的裝置
  • 治療與用藥、檢驗結果、副作用、約診、醫療團隊聯絡人，以及想問醫師的問題
  • 應用程式設定：提醒時間、單位、語言、文字大小、高對比、減少動態效果及應用程式鎖定偏好

此健康紀錄不會上傳給我們。Oncora 沒有帳號系統，我們不會收集您的姓名、電子郵件、電話號碼或其他個人識別資訊。

2. 無需帳號或登入

Oncora 不要求您建立帳號或登入即可追蹤健康狀況。我們絕不出售您的資訊。

3. AI 夥伴

Oncora 提供選用的 AI 夥伴。當您傳送訊息時，該訊息會傳送至我們的伺服器，再轉送給第三方 AI 服務商以生成回覆。請避免輸入您不希望以此方式處理的內容。若您不使用 AI 夥伴，則不會傳送任何訊息。我們不會使用您儲存的健康紀錄來訓練任何 AI 模型。

4. 廣告

Oncora 可能在部分畫面顯示由 Google AdMob 提供的橫幅廣告。我們僅請求非個人化廣告——廣告不會基於跨應用程式追蹤或您的健康資料。為了投放及評估廣告，AdMob 可能會處理有限的技術資料，例如您裝置的 IP 位址及一般裝置資訊。我們不會在記錄流程或 AI 夥伴中顯示廣告。AdMob 如何處理資料，請參閱 Google 的隱私權政策。

5. 備份與還原

您可以將所有資料匯出為 JSON 檔案，並透過貼上備份內容或選擇備份檔案在另一台裝置上還原。匯出及裝置上的自動快照皆在您的裝置本機處理。匯出的檔案可能包含您的健康資訊——離開應用程式後請妥善保管。

6. 您選擇分享的報告

您可以產生 PDF 摘要或照護者更新，與您的醫療團隊或家人分享。這些內容在裝置本機建立，只有在您明確選擇分享時，才會透過系統分享選單離開應用程式。

7. 通知

Oncora 可發送本機提醒，包括記錄、約診、用藥及週期中段（低點）血球數提醒。通知完全在裝置本機排程，不會向任何伺服器傳送通知資料。

8. 資料刪除

您可隨時在應用程式中刪除個別資料。解除安裝應用程式即可移除裝置上所有本機儲存的資料。沒有任何雲端的健康紀錄副本需要刪除。

9. 兒童隱私

Oncora 並非針對 13 歲以下兒童設計。若您認為有兒童輸入了個人健康資訊，請於應用程式內移除，或解除安裝應用程式。

10. 聯絡方式

如有疑問或意見，請聯絡我們：wksunshine@gmail.com。

Oncora 旨在協助您進行自我追蹤，並與您的醫療提供者溝通。它並非醫療器材，亦不提供醫療建議、診斷或治療。有關健康問題，請務必諮詢合格的醫療專業人員。
