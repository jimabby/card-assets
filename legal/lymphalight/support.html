Frequently Asked Questions

Is my data private?

Yes. LymphaLight is completely local-first — all your data is stored only on your device. There is no account, no server, and no cloud. Nothing leaves your phone unless you explicitly export a backup.

How do I back up my data?

Go to Settings → Export Backup. This creates a JSON file containing all your check-ins and node logs. Save it somewhere safe — iCloud Drive, email, or another secure location. To restore on a new device, go to Settings → Restore and paste the backup content.

How do I share a report with my doctor?

Go to the Report tab and tap Generate Report. LymphaLight creates a PDF summary of your check-in history and node measurements. Use the system share sheet to AirDrop, email, or print the report.

How do I log a lymph node?

Go to the Nodes tab and tap on a region on the body diagram. Select the specific node location, enter the size measurement (in mm or cm), and describe how it feels. You can add multiple entries over time to track changes.

How do I set a daily reminder?

Go to Settings → Reminder and toggle on daily notifications. Choose your preferred time. The reminder is a local notification — no internet connection is needed.

How do I delete my data?

Go to Settings → Clear All Data to permanently remove all check-ins, node logs, and settings from your device. Uninstalling the app also removes all data. There is no cloud copy to delete.

What is LymphaLight used for?

LymphaLight helps people living with lymphoma track their lymph nodes and symptoms over time. You can log daily check-ins, monitor how individual nodes change, and generate reports to share with your care team.

LymphaLight is intended to support self-tracking and conversations with your healthcare providers. It is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Always consult a qualified healthcare professional about your health.
