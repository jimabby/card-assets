Delete Your Hiro Account

How to delete your Hiro cloud account and data.

1. Delete Your Account in the App

If you use cloud mode, you can delete your account and all synced data directly inside the app:

  1. Open Hiro Mobile and go to the Settings tab.
  2. Tap Delete account.
  3. Confirm the deletion.

Deletion takes effect immediately and cannot be undone.

2. What Gets Deleted

  • Your cloud account (the email/password sign-in for your Supabase project).
  • Every job application record synced to the cloud — statuses, notes, match scores, and related data.

Not affected: the data stored locally by the Hiro desktop app on your own computer. That data belongs to you and stays on your machine; remove it by deleting it in the desktop app or uninstalling it.

3. Wi-Fi Mode — No Account Exists

If you only use Wi-Fi (LAN) mode, there is no account and no cloud data. The app stores only your connection settings on your device — disconnecting or uninstalling the app removes them.

4. Need Help?

If you can't access the app or run into any issue deleting your account, email us at wksunshine@gmail.com and we'll help you complete the deletion.

Note: because your data lives in a Supabase project that you own, you can also delete the entire project yourself from the Supabase dashboard, which removes everything at once.

刪除您的 Hiro 帳號

如何刪除您的 Hiro 雲端帳號及資料。

1. 在應用程式內刪除帳號

若您使用雲端模式，可直接在應用程式內刪除帳號及所有同步資料：

  1. 開啟 Hiro Mobile，前往設定分頁。
  2. 點選刪除帳號。
  3. 確認刪除。

刪除會立即生效，且無法復原。

2. 哪些資料會被刪除

  • 您的雲端帳號（用於登入您 Supabase 專案的電子郵件／密碼）。
  • 所有同步至雲端的求職應徵記錄 — 狀態、備注、匹配分數及相關資料。

不受影響：Hiro 桌面應用程式儲存在您電腦上的本機資料。這些資料屬於您並保留在您的電腦上；如需移除，請在桌面應用程式中刪除或解除安裝該程式。

3. Wi-Fi 模式 — 沒有帳號

若您僅使用 Wi-Fi（區域網路）模式，則不存在帳號或雲端資料。應用程式僅在您的裝置上儲存連線設定 — 中斷連線或解除安裝應用程式即可移除。

4. 需要協助？

若您無法存取應用程式，或在刪除帳號時遇到任何問題，請發送郵件至 wksunshine@gmail.com，我們會協助您完成刪除。

提示：由於您的資料存放在由您自己擁有的 Supabase 專案中，您也可以直接在 Supabase 控制台刪除整個專案，一次移除所有資料。
