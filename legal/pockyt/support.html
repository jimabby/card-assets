Frequently Asked Questions

How do I set up the AI assistant?

There's nothing to set up — the AI assistant is built in, with no API key to manage. Just open the AI tab and start chatting. Pockyt's AI is powered by Google Gemini and works out of the box. Free accounts include a monthly allowance of AI chats and receipt scans; you can raise the limit by upgrading to Pro in Settings.

How do I enable cloud sync?

Sign in with Google, Apple, or email from Settings. Once signed in, your data automatically syncs across your devices via Firebase. You can disable or delete your cloud data at any time from Settings.

How do I import transactions from my bank?

Go to Settings → Import CSV. Export a CSV from your bank (Date, Type, Category, Amount, Note columns) and paste it into the import screen. Pockyt will preview the entries before saving.

How do I scan a receipt?

Tap the camera icon when adding a transaction, or use the dedicated Scan tab. Receipt scanning uses the built-in AI — no setup required. Free accounts include a set number of scans each month; upgrade to Pro in Settings for a higher limit.

How does the cashback optimizer work?

When you add a transaction and select a category, Pockyt checks your saved cards and highlights if a different card earns higher cashback for that spending category. No external data is sent — this is computed entirely on your device.

How do I split a bill?

Go to the Bill Split tab, create a new split with the total and participants, then divide evenly or enter custom amounts per person. Mark participants as settled when they pay you back.

Can I export my data?

Yes. Go to Settings → Export to download your data as JSON (full backup) or CSV (spreadsheet-friendly). You can also generate a PDF report with optional date range filtering.

How do I delete my account and data?

To delete your cloud data and account, go to Settings → Account → Delete Account. Local data is removed when you uninstall the app. You can also email us at wksunshine@gmail.com and we'll handle deletion manually.

Is my data safe?

All data is stored locally on your device by default. Cloud sync is optional and protected by Firebase security rules — only you can access your data. We never sell or share your financial data with third parties.

常见问题

如何设置 AI 助手？

无需任何设置 — AI 助手已内置，也无需管理 API 密钥。直接打开 AI 标签即可开始对话。Pockyt 的 AI 由 Google Gemini 提供支持，开箱即用。免费账户每月包含一定额度的 AI 对话和收据扫描次数；如需更高额度，可在"设置"中升级到 Pro。

如何启用云同步？

在"设置"中使用 Google、Apple 或电子邮件登录。登录后，数据将通过 Firebase 自动同步到您的所有设备。您可随时在"设置"中停用或删除云端数据。

如何从银行导入交易记录？

前往"设置 → 导入 CSV"。从您的银行导出 CSV 文件（包含日期、类型、分类、金额、备注列），粘贴到导入界面。Pockyt 将在保存前预览所有条目。

如何扫描收据？

添加交易时点击相机图标，或使用专属的"扫描"标签。收据扫描使用内置 AI，无需任何设置。免费账户每月包含一定次数的扫描；如需更多，可在"设置"中升级到 Pro。

返现优化提示如何使用？

添加交易并选择分类时，Pockyt 会检查您保存的信用卡，提示是否有其他卡片在该消费分类下提供更高返现。此功能完全在设备本地计算，不发送任何外部数据。

如何分摊账单？

前往"账单分摊"标签，创建新的分摊（填写总金额和参与者），然后平均分配或为每位参与者输入自定义金额。对方还款后将其标记为已结清。

能导出数据吗？

可以。前往"设置 → 导出"，将数据下载为 JSON（完整备份）或 CSV（表格格式）。您也可以生成支持日期范围筛选的 PDF 报告。

如何删除账户和数据？

前往"设置 → 账户 → 删除账户"可删除云端数据及账户。卸载应用可清除本地数据。您也可以发送邮件至 wksunshine@gmail.com，我们将手动处理删除请求。

我的数据安全吗？

默认情况下，所有数据存储在您的本地设备上。云同步为可选项，受 Firebase 安全规则保护 — 只有您本人可以访问自己的数据。我们不会向任何第三方出售或共享您的财务数据。
