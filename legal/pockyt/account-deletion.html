Delete Your Pockyt Account

Pockyt — by Cessleigh Pty Ltd. How to delete your account and data.

1. Delete Your Account in the App

You can delete your Pockyt account and all associated data directly inside the app:

  1. Open Pockyt and go to the Settings tab.
  2. Scroll to the account section and tap Delete Account.
  3. Confirm the deletion. You may be asked to sign in again to verify it's you.

This works for accounts created with Google, Apple, or email/password, as well as anonymous accounts.

2. What Is Deleted

When you delete your account, the following data is permanently removed:

  • Your account and sign-in credentials (Firebase Authentication record).
  • All cloud-synced data: transactions, cards, budgets, goals, subscriptions, settings, and AI chat history.
  • Your household membership and any household you own, including its shared data.
  • Your trial and subscription entitlement records on our servers (your App Store / Google Play subscription itself must be cancelled with the store — deleting your account does not cancel store billing).

Data stored locally on your device is also cleared. If you only want to wipe local data without deleting your account, use Settings → Clear All Data.

3. Processing Time

In-app deletion takes effect immediately. Residual copies in encrypted backups of our cloud infrastructure are purged within 30 days. We do not retain your financial data after this window, except where retention is required by law.

4. Can't Access the App?

If you no longer have the app installed or can't sign in, email us a deletion request at wksunshine@gmail.com from the email address associated with your account (or include your sign-in method). We will verify your identity and complete the deletion within 30 days, and confirm by reply.

Last updated: June 2026 · See also our Privacy Policy

删除您的 Pockyt 账户

Pockyt — Cessleigh Pty Ltd 出品。如何删除您的账户和数据。

1. 在应用内删除账户

您可以直接在应用内删除 Pockyt 账户及所有关联数据：

  1. 打开 Pockyt，进入设置标签页。
  2. 滚动至账户部分，点击删除账户。
  3. 确认删除。为验证身份，系统可能要求您重新登录。

此操作适用于通过 Google、Apple 或电子邮件/密码创建的账户，以及匿名账户。

2. 删除的数据范围

删除账户后，以下数据将被永久移除：

  • 您的账户及登录凭据（Firebase Authentication 记录）。
  • 所有云同步数据：交易记录、卡片、预算、目标、订阅、设置及 AI 对话历史。
  • 您的家庭组成员资格，以及您拥有的家庭组及其共享数据。
  • 我们服务器上的试用及订阅资格记录（App Store / Google Play 的订阅本身需在相应商店取消——删除账户不会取消商店扣费）。

设备本地存储的数据也将被清除。如只想清除本地数据而保留账户，请使用设置 → 清除所有数据。

3. 处理时限

应用内删除立即生效。云基础设施加密备份中的残留副本将在 30 天内清除。除法律要求保留的情况外，超过此期限后我们不再保留您的财务数据。

4. 无法访问应用？

如果您已卸载应用或无法登录，请使用与账户关联的邮箱（或注明您的登录方式）发送删除请求至 wksunshine@gmail.com。我们将在核实身份后于 30 天内完成删除，并回复确认。
