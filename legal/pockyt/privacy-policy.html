Pockyt Privacy Policy

Your financial data, your privacy.

1. Information We Collect

Pockyt stores your financial data — transactions, cards, budgets, goals, recurring entries, subscriptions, and bill splits — locally on your device. Pro subscribers may enable optional cloud sync, which stores your data in Firebase Firestore to enable cross-device access. We do not sell or share your personal financial data with third parties.

2. AI Features

Pockyt supports AI-powered receipt scanning and a conversational AI assistant. These features are powered by Google Gemini. No API key is required from you — the integration is managed by Pockyt.

When you use an AI feature, relevant data (receipt images, transaction context, or your chat messages) is sent to Google Gemini and is subject to Google's Privacy Policy. Pockyt does not store or log this data on its own servers.

3. Authentication

Pockyt uses Firebase Authentication. You may use the app entirely without signing in — all core features work offline with local storage. You may optionally sign in with Google, Apple, or email/password to enable cloud sync and cross-device access.

4. Camera & Photos

Pockyt requests camera and photo library access solely for receipt capture. Receipt images are processed on-device or sent to Google Gemini for OCR; they are not stored by Pockyt's servers. You may attach receipt photos to transactions; these are stored locally on your device.

5. Location Data

Pockyt uses your device's approximate location (coarse location) to automatically determine whether you are in mainland China or another region. This selection directs your data to the appropriate cloud infrastructure and AI routing path. No precise GPS coordinates are collected or stored. Location data is not shared with third parties.

6. Advertising Identifier

Pockyt may access your device's advertising identifier (IDFA on iOS, or the equivalent on Android) for analytics and to understand the effectiveness of our marketing. You may opt out at any time through your device's privacy settings (on iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking).

7. Notifications

Pockyt may send local push notifications to remind you of upcoming credit card payment due dates and recurring transaction reminders. These notifications are scheduled entirely on-device and no notification content is transmitted to any server.

8. Local-Only Features

The following features compute everything on your device and send no data externally:

  • Spending heatmap and category breakdowns
  • Cash flow forecast
  • Bill splitting
  • Subscription management
  • Merchant auto-categorization rules
  • Cashback optimizer hints
  • CSV import and PDF export
  • Budget tracking and savings goals

9. Data Security

Cloud-synced data is protected by Firebase security rules that ensure only you can access your account. You can delete all your cloud data at any time from Settings. Local data can be cleared by deleting the app.

10. Regional Availability

Pockyt operates globally. Cloud sync and AI features may be restricted or unavailable in mainland China due to local data protection and internet regulations. We are working to bring compliant versions of these features to users in China.

11. Bank Integration (Coming Soon)

A future update will allow you to securely link bank accounts for automatic transaction import via a regulated open banking API with read-only access. Pockyt will never store your banking credentials. A full privacy policy update will be provided before this feature launches.

12. Contact

For questions, feedback, or data deletion requests, please contact us at wksunshine@gmail.com.

Pockyt 隱私政策

您的財務資料，由您掌控。

1. 我們收集的資訊

Pockyt 將您的財務資料（包括交易紀錄、信用卡、預算、儲蓄目標、定期項目、訂閱及帳單分攤）儲存於您的本機裝置。Pro 訂閱用戶可選擇啟用雲端同步功能，將資料安全儲存於 Firebase Firestore，以實現跨裝置存取。我們不會向第三方出售或分享您的個人財務資料。

2. AI 功能

Pockyt 支援 AI 收據掃描和對話式 AI 助手。這些功能由 Google Gemini 提供支援，無需您自行提供 API 金鑰，相關整合由 Pockyt 統一管理。

使用 AI 功能時，相關資料（收據圖片、交易情境或對話內容）將傳送至 Google Gemini，並受 Google 隱私政策的約束。Pockyt 不會在自身伺服器上儲存或記錄這些資料。

3. 身份驗證

Pockyt 使用 Firebase Authentication 進行身份驗證。您無需登入即可使用所有核心功能，資料完全離線儲存於本機。您也可以選擇透過 Google、Apple 或電子郵件/密碼登入，以啟用雲端同步和跨裝置存取。

4. 相機與照片

Pockyt 僅為拍攝收據請求相機和相簿存取權限。收據圖片在裝置本機處理，或傳送至 Google Gemini 進行文字辨識；Pockyt 伺服器不會儲存這些圖片。附加至交易紀錄的收據照片儲存於您的本機裝置。

5. 位置資料

Pockyt 使用您裝置的大致位置（粗略定位）自動判斷您是否位於中國大陸。此判斷用於將您的資料導向至對應的雲端基礎設施和 AI 路徑。應用程式不會採集或儲存精確 GPS 座標，位置資料也不會與第三方分享。

6. 廣告識別碼

Pockyt 可能會存取您裝置的廣告識別碼（iOS 上的 IDFA，或 Android 上的同類識別碼），用於應用程式分析及行銷成效衡量。您可隨時在裝置隱私設定中選擇退出（iOS：設定 → 隱私與安全性 → 追蹤）。

7. 通知

Pockyt 可能會發送本機推播通知，提醒您即將到來的信用卡還款日和定期交易。這些通知完全在裝置本機排程，不會向任何伺服器傳輸通知內容。

8. 純本機功能

以下功能僅在您的裝置上進行計算，不向外部傳送任何資料：

  • 消費熱力圖及分類統計
  • 現金流預測
  • 帳單分攤
  • 訂閱管理
  • 商家自動分類規則
  • 現金回饋最佳化提示
  • CSV 匯入及 PDF 匯出
  • 預算追蹤及儲蓄目標

9. 資料安全

雲端同步資料受 Firebase 安全規則保護，確保只有您本人才能存取自己的帳戶。您可隨時在「設定」中刪除所有雲端資料。本機資料可透過解除安裝應用程式清除。

10. 地區可用性

Pockyt 面向全球用戶提供服務。由於當地資料保護和網路法規，雲端同步及 AI 功能在中國大陸可能受到限制或無法使用。我們正積極推進合規版本的開發，以服務中國用戶。

11. 銀行整合（即將推出）

未來版本將支援透過受監管的開放銀行 API 以唯讀方式安全關聯銀行帳戶，實現自動匯入交易紀錄。Pockyt 絕不會儲存您的銀行憑證。功能上線前，我們將提供完整的隱私政策更新說明。

12. 聯絡方式

如有疑問、意見或資料刪除請求，請發送郵件至 wksunshine@gmail.com。
