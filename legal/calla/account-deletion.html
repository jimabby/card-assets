Delete Your Calla Account

How to permanently delete your account and health data.

1. Delete Your Account in the App

You can delete your account and all of your data directly inside the app:

  1. Open Calla and go to Settings (the gear on the Home screen).
  2. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete account & data.
  3. Confirm the deletion. For security you may be asked to re-enter your password or re-confirm your Apple or Google sign-in.

Deletion takes effect immediately and cannot be undone.

2. What Gets Deleted

  • Your account (email/password, Apple, or Google sign-in).
  • Every record in your account — mood check-ins, symptoms, vital readings (including any imported from Apple Health or Health Connect), medications and dose history, appointments, journal entries, care-circle contacts, AI chat history, emergency info, and preferences.

Not affected: the data in Apple Health or Health Connect itself — Calla only ever reads from those services, so your original health records stay untouched on your device.

3. Demo Mode — No Account Exists

If you only tried Calla in demo mode, there is no account and no cloud data. Demo entries live on your device only and are discarded when you leave the demo or uninstall the app.

4. Need Help?

If you can't access the app or run into any issue deleting your account, email us at wksunshine@gmail.com from the address you signed up with, and we'll complete the deletion for you.

刪除您的 Calla 帳號

如何永久刪除您的帳號及健康資料。

1. 在應用程式內刪除帳號

您可直接在應用程式內刪除帳號及所有資料：

  1. 開啟 Calla，前往設定（主畫面上的齒輪）。
  2. 捲動至底部，點選刪除帳號與資料。
  3. 確認刪除。為安全起見，系統可能要求您重新輸入密碼，或重新確認您的 Apple 或 Google 登入。

刪除會立即生效，且無法復原。

2. 哪些資料會被刪除

  • 您的帳號（電子郵件密碼、Apple 或 Google 登入）。
  • 帳號中的每一筆記錄 — 心情打卡、症狀、生命體徵（包括從 Apple 健康或 Health Connect 匯入的讀數）、藥物及服藥記錄、預約、日記、關懷圈聯絡人、AI 聊天記錄、緊急資訊及偏好設定。

不受影響：Apple 健康或 Health Connect 本身的資料 — Calla 只會讀取這些服務，您原始的健康記錄會完整保留在您的裝置上。

3. 示範模式 — 沒有帳號

若您僅以示範模式試用 Calla，則不存在帳號或雲端資料。示範記錄僅存在於您的裝置上，離開示範模式或解除安裝應用程式時即會清除。

4. 需要協助？

若您無法存取應用程式，或在刪除帳號時遇到任何問題，請使用註冊時的電子郵件地址發送郵件至 wksunshine@gmail.com，我們會協助您完成刪除。
